Part 1. Two families of heat

Star the three methods you have used yourself.

Note on frying: we do not fry in this room. Frying needs oil at 350 degrees or more, and a pot of hot oil is the one hazard this course does not take on. Everything a fryer does, an oven or a broiler can get close to.
