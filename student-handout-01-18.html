Back of the sheet: the visual plan template

Draw a paper plate. Divide it into the five MyPlate sections (fruits and vegetables on one half; grains and protein on the other half; a small circle for dairy beside it). Write the dish in each section and its cost under it. Write the running total in a box at the bottom. Plan on the plate first if that is easier, then copy to the table.
