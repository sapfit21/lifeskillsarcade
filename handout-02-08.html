Part C: Be the detective

Show your math in the boxes.

  1. Gross pay. Hours x rate = gross.

______ hours x $______ = $______ Does it match line 1? yes / no

  1. Total deductions. Add lines 2, 3, 4, and 5.

$______ + $______ + $______ + $______ = $______

  1. Net pay. Gross minus total deductions.

$______ minus $______ = $______ Does it match line 6? yes / no

  1. Percent taken out. Total deductions divided by gross, times 100. Round to a whole number.

$______ divided by $______ = ______ x 100 = ______ percent

(Grade 6 short version: do items 1, 2, and 3 and questions 5 and 6 only.)

Detective questions

  1. Which deduction is the largest? _________________________ How do you know? ______________________
  2. Dana gets 26 of these paychecks in a year. What is her net pay for the year? Show the math.

26 x $______ = $______

  1. Dana pays her own phone plan, $35 a month, and a monthly bus pass, $60. She gets two paychecks a month. Does her take-home cover those two needs? Write two sentences with the numbers.
  1. Dana's friend earns the same $17 an hour but works 30 hours in two weeks. Without doing all the math, will the friend's Social Security deduction be larger, smaller, or the same? Why?

Grade 8 stretch

  1. Check the stub for errors. 6.2 percent of $408 = $______. 1.45 percent of $408 = $______. Do lines 4 and 5 match?
  2. About 17 percent comes out of every paycheck. What gross pay does Dana need in two weeks to take home $500? (Hint: net is 83 percent of gross.)

$500 divided by 0.83 = $______

  1. A job posting says "$52,000 a year, paid every two weeks." What is the gross pay per paycheck? $52,000 divided by ______ paychecks = $______

Exit card (3-2-1)
