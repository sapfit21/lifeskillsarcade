Lesson 1.22, one day, 9 slides. The test slides stay up for the last twenty minutes.

Slide 1

Do now

  • Name one person you know who works with food for a living.
  • What is their job called?

Teacher notes

Five quick answers on the board.

Slide 2

Every job you named is on a card

  • So are five you have never heard of.
  • One of them pays more than a lot of doctors' offices.
  • You can start it in this kitchen.
Slide 3

The food career ladder

  • Dishwasher, prep cook, line cook: no degree. Start from the bottom, climb by showing up and learning.
  • Baker, restaurant manager, caterer: a certificate, an apprenticeship, or a two-year degree.
  • School food service director, health inspector, food scientist, registered dietitian: a four-year degree or more.
  • Pay ranges on the cards are to be refreshed before teaching.

Teacher notes

Image: a ladder drawn with the ten careers on rungs, color coded in three groups.

Ask: which of these did you do a piece of in this unit? All of them.

Slide 4

The card sort

  • Pairs. Ten cards. Ten minutes.
  • Sort 1: by education needed. Name one job per group.
  • Sort 2: mostly food, mostly people, or mostly data?
  • Sort 3: by pay, lowest to highest. Circle the surprise.
Slide 5

While you sort, ask

  • Which job needs the most school? Which needs none past high school?
  • A health inspector and a line cook both care about the danger zone. How is their day different?
  • Where does a school food service director fit?
Slide 6

Would I like this?

  • Pick two cards.
  • The day I would rather have. The education I am willing to do. The pay I would need.
  • One sentence: "I would or would not like ___ because ___."
  • Keep it in your FACS folder. Unit 2 and Unit 7 use it.

Teacher notes

3 minutes. Not graded for the answer, only for being done.

Slide 7

Unit 1 test

  • Twenty items. Twenty minutes. Fifty points.
  • Part 1: Safe and Clean. Part 2: Tools, Measuring, Recipes. Part 3: Nutrients, MyPlate, Labels. Part 4: Plan, Shop, Cook.
  • Calculators for items 8 and 17 only. Show your work on both.
  • If you are not sure, write what you think and why.
Slide 8

Timer

  • 20:00

Leave this slide up. Collect at the bell.

Slide 9

On the way out

  • Say one word: the career you picked on the self-check.
  • Unit 1 is done. Tomorrow: Unit 2, who I am, my goals, my money.
