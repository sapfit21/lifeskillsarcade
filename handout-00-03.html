The situation

Read this with me. It is made up, but the cold is real.

You and the three other people at your table were on a small plane. It was flying over the north woods in late October. The engine failed. The pilot made a hard landing on the shore of a big lake. Everyone walked away with scrapes and bruises, nothing worse. The plane is wrecked and will not fly again. But it is in one piece, and the door still closes.

Before the landing, the pilot radioed a distress call and gave the plane's position. The radio is dead now. But searchers know the general area, about 20 miles across. The nearest town is 40 miles away through thick forest with no road. It is about 35 degrees F in the daytime and drops below freezing at night. The lake water is clean-looking but not safe to drink without boiling. It is 2 in the afternoon, and dark comes at 6.

Ten things survived the crash. Your job: rank them from 1 to 10. Number 1 is the most important for staying alive until searchers find you. Number 10 is the least important.

Part 2: Rank as a team (talk)

Rule: Your team makes one ranking. Every person must be able to say why for the top three. No voting on the whole list. Talk through each item. Move the cards on the table. Write the team ranking in the Team column.

Sentence starter: "I think ___ is number ___ because ___."

Part 3: Score

Directions: Copy the expert ranking into the Expert column as I reveal it. Then score your Alone column. Go item by item. Find item A in your Alone column and in the Expert column. Subtract the smaller rank number from the bigger one. Write the answer in the Alone difference column, on the row where you wrote A. Do all ten items. Add up the column. Then do the same for the Team column.

Example: You put the matches at 4. The expert put them at 1. The difference is 3.
