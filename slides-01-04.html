Slide outline for Lesson 1.4, two days. Day 1 is eleven slides (1 to 11). Day 2 is seventeen slides (12 to 28) because the review quiz show runs one question per slide; slides 15 to 26 are the twelve quiz questions and reuse the six station photos from Slides 01.01 - Hunt the Hazard.md.

Day 1

Slide 1

Our Kitchen Safety Contract

  • Lesson 1.4: Safe and Clean
  • Today: we write the rules, we vote, we sign
  • Tomorrow: the exam. Pass it and you cook next week.

Teacher notes

Image: a blank poster with a title line and a row of signature lines.

Leave up while students enter. Hand each team its stack: Hazard Hunt ranking, Fix It strip, relay answer sheet.

Slide 2

Do now

  • Look at your team's top five from the Hazard Hunt
  • Which one is still the most likely to happen in this room next week?
  • Write it and one reason. Two minutes.

Teacher notes

One hazard from each team on the board. Then the story on the next slide.

Slide 3

The room we share

  • This room is used by more than one class
  • One weekend: grease left on the stove, food left on the counters
  • The next class walked into a room that smelled and a counter they could not use
  • Nobody got hurt. Nobody could cook either.
  • Today we write the rules so that never happens to the class after us
Slide 4

The sanitation routine

  • Start: hair back, sleeves up, wash hands, sanitize surfaces
  • During: clean as you go
  • End: station check before dismissal
  • Every lab. Every time. Not up for a vote.

Teacher notes

Image: a six-picture strip: a hair tie, a pushed-up sleeve, hands under a faucet, a spray bottle and cloth, a wiped counter mid-lab, a teacher with a checklist at a station.

Do the routine at a station in real time while you talk. Then the class says the six steps back on fingers. Students copy the routine into the box on the contract template.

Slide 5

Station check

  • Counter wiped and sanitized
  • Sink empty and wiped
  • Stove off and wiped
  • Towels hung, sponges rinsed
  • Trash in, tools away, chairs in

Teacher notes

This is what I look at before anyone leaves. Nobody is dismissed until every station passes.

Slide 6

What a contract is

  • Rules we write ourselves
  • Rules we vote on
  • Rules we sign
  • Rules we can check by looking

Teacher notes

The difference from the Room and Lab Agreement in Unit 0: that one is my rules for the room; this one is your rules for the kitchen.

Slide 7

Your team writes three rules

  • Each rule starts "We will"
  • One sentence. Name the hazard it prevents in parentheses.
  • One rule from your Hazard Hunt top five
  • One from Hands, Zone, or Cross
  • One from fire, burns, cuts, or tools
  • One rule per sticky note. 10 minutes.

Teacher notes

Frame for ELL students printed on the pad: "We will ___ so that nobody ___." A rule like "We will be safe" gets the question "safe from what?" Grade 6 teams write two. Grade 8 teams write a fourth about what happens when a rule is broken.

Slide 8

Merge and vote

  • Every note gets read aloud
  • Same idea: same group
  • For each group: what hazard does it stop? Can I check it by looking?
  • A rule needs more than half the room
  • Cap: ten rules

Teacher notes

Group duplicates on the board as you read. Vote by hand. Write the final list on the poster as you go.

Slide 9

Our contract

  • The final eight to ten rules go here, typed in after the vote

Teacher notes

Read the contract aloud once. Students copy the final rules onto their own templates.

Slide 10

Sign

  • I sign first
  • Then every team comes up
  • Sign your own copy too
  • Take it home. The family line is optional.

Teacher notes

Sign the poster in front of them before any student does. Then call teams up.

Slide 11

Exit card and tomorrow

  • 3 steps of the sanitation routine
  • 2 contract rules you think will be hardest to keep
  • 1 question you want answered before the exam
  • Tomorrow: review game, then the Kitchen Safety Exam
  • Review: the six families, Hands, Zone, Cross, fire, burn, cut, the routine

Day 2

Teacher notes

The "1 question" lines become extra quiz show slides tomorrow.

Slide 12

Exam day

  • Lesson 1.4, Day 2
  • Twelve questions, four teams, then the exam
  • Pass it and you cook next week

Teacher notes

Image: the signed contract poster.

Leave up while students enter. Whiteboards and markers on every team table.

Slide 13

Do now

  • Put the six steps of the sanitation routine in order
  • Start: ___, ___, ___, ___. During: ___. End: ___.
  • Word bank (scrambled): clean as you go, wash hands, station check, sleeves up, sanitize surfaces, hair back

Teacher notes

Read the correct order from the poster as a class.

Slide 14

Quiz show rules

  • Write your answer on the whiteboard
  • Hold it up on three
  • One point per correct board
  • I read the right answer after every slide
  • Correct your own mistakes out loud

Teacher notes

Keep it fast. Twelve slides in twelve minutes. Ask the two "needs more" questions twice.

Slide 15

Station 1

  • Name the hazard and its family

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 1 photo from Slides 1.1 (pot handle out, cord on the burner).

Answer: burns (handle), electrical (cord). One point for either with the right family.

Slide 16

Station 2

  • Name the hazard and its family

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 2 photo from Slides 1.1 (wet towel on the floor, cabinet open).

Answer: falls (towel).

Slide 17

Station 3

  • Name the hazard and its family

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 3 photo from Slides 1.1 (shears under suds).

Answer: cuts.

Slide 18

Station 4

  • Name the hazard and its family. Bonus: what is it called?

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 4 photo from Slides 1.1 (raw chicken touching bread).

Answer: food contamination; cross contamination.

Slide 19

Station 5

  • Name the hazard and its family. Bonus: where would you look up what is in the bottle?

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 5 photo from Slides 1.1 (unlabeled bottle, mitt with a hole).

Answer: contamination or chemical (bottle), burns (mitt); the Safety Data Sheets.

Slide 20

Station 6

  • Name the hazard and its family

Teacher notes

Image: the Station 6 photo from Slides 1.1 (paper towel on the burner, wet mitt).

Answer: fire (paper towel), burns (wet mitt).

Slide 21

Hands

  • How many seconds do you scrub?
  • Name two times you must wash your hands in a kitchen

Teacher notes

Answer: 20 seconds; any two from the list (before food, after raw meat or eggs, after the bathroom, after your face, hair, or phone, after coughing or sneezing, after the trash).

Slide 22

Zone

  • What are the two temperatures of the danger zone?
  • What is the two-hour rule?

Teacher notes

Answer: 40 F and 140 F; food in the zone more than two hours gets thrown out. Ask this one twice if the exit cards say so.

Slide 23

Cross

  • The cutting board touched raw egg. Then it touched bread. What just happened?
  • Fix it in one sentence.

Teacher notes

Answer: cross contamination; wash and sanitize the board or use a second board.

Slide 24

Cross

  • A sponge wiped raw chicken juice, then wiped the table you eat at. Safe or not safe? Why?

Teacher notes

Answer: not safe; the sponge carries the bacteria to the eating surface. Paper towel for raw juice, then sanitize.

Slide 25

Fire

  • Oil in the pan is on fire. Three things you do, in order. One thing you never do.

Teacher notes

Answer: turn off the burner, lid on from the side, get back and tell the teacher; never water. Ask this one twice if the exit cards say so.

Slide 26

Burn

  • You touched a hot pan. What do you do first, for how long, and what do you never put on it?

Teacher notes

Answer: cool running water, at least 10 minutes, never ice or butter, then tell the teacher.

Slide 27

The Kitchen Safety Exam

  • 15 questions, 30 points, 20 minutes
  • 24 points to pass (80 percent)
  • Retakes allowed as many times as you need
  • Nobody cooks until they pass
  • Finished early? Read the contract on the wall.

Teacher notes

Separate desks. Read the directions aloud. Timer on. Collect as students finish.

Slide 28

After the exam

  • On a slip: "The one rule I will follow at home this week is ___."
  • Scores come back next period
  • Pass: Lesson 1.5, Tools and Measuring
  • Under 24: ten minutes with me, then the retake during the next do now

Teacher notes

Two students read their slips aloud. Leave this up while they pack.
