Name: ______________________ Period: ______ Investor number: ______ Start date: __________

This journal stays in your FACS folder for the whole Gilded Age Market. You write one entry every class day. Every entry has three parts and one number:

  • What I did (bought, sold, or held; which company; how many shares)
  • Why I did it (a dispatch, a risk level, my pie, a feeling you noticed)
  • What happened (did the price move, did my total move)
  • My total at the bell, in dollars (the number on your ledger)

Three sentences and the number is a complete entry. More is welcome.

Sentence starters:

Today I bought ___ because ___.Hoy compré ___ porque ___.
