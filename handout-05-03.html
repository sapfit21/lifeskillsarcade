Part 2: The station record

Three tests on six swatches. Circle or write in every box. Ninety seconds per swatch.

Test 1, feel: rub it between finger and thumb. Circle one in each pair.
Test 2, stretch: pull gently across, then let go. Did it come back?
Test 3, water drop: one drop, count to ten. Soaked in, beaded up, or spread out?
