Part 4: Checklist (how this card is scored)

The career research card is the formal check for Topic 2.5. Score: 0 to 4.

PointEarned when
1The career is from one of my top three clusters and the cluster number is written.
1Three tasks, the path, and the pay range with a source year are filled from a named source, not guessed.
1A day in the life has three lines that say where, who with, and what the day feels like.
1The verdict names my two letters or my values and gives one reason for and one reason against.

Grade 6 support: two tasks and one "day in the life" line; the verdict is one sentence.
