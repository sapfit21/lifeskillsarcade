Page 1: The 30 food cards (cut apart)

Page 3: Lifespan needs table

Directions: Three people, three different bodies. For each person, pick the TWO nutrients they need most right now and write one sentence why, using a job from the chart on page 2.

Nutrient bank to circle or write: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water
