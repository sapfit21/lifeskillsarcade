## What this class is

Family and Consumer Sciences is the class where you learn to run your own life. You will cook real food in a real kitchen. You will sew something you can use. You will build a budget, invest in a simulated market, design a room, plan meals for a family, take care of a younger child safely, and pitch a business idea to a panel. Every unit ends with something you made.

I teach this class because these are the skills I wish someone had handed me at 12. I spent years in business, finance, and hospitality before I became a teacher, and I use all of it here.

- A folder that stays in the room (I provide it)

Labs and projects are scored with a rubric you see before you start. You can retake any quiz once after a review. If you miss a lab, you do a written alternative so you do not lose points for being absent. Late work is accepted; see the grading plan on the website for the details.

You cannot cook until you pass the Kitchen Safety Exam. You can retake it as many times as you need.

## How the room works

1. The do now is on the board when you walk in. Sit down, start writing.
2. In a lab, everyone has a role card. Do your role. Then help.
3. When you hear the reset cue (lights, countdown, hands empty), stop, look, listen.
4. Clean up is part of the lab. Nobody leaves until the station passes the check.
5. Phones stay put away unless I say otherwise. Some lessons use them.
6. We talk about food, bodies, families, and money in this class. Be respectful. Nobody is required to share anything personal.

The full Room and Lab Agreement is the first thing we read and sign together.

## Food allergies and food rules

Tell me on the first day, in writing, about any allergy or food rule (religious, medical, or family). I plan every lab around what is on that list. Recipes are posted a week ahead so you and your family can check them. No lab requires you to eat.

## Language support

If English is not your first language, you will get vocabulary cards and sentence starters in your language for every unit (currently Turkish, Portuguese, and Spanish; ask me for others). Every lab has picture step cards. Slides are posted for everyone.

## Technology and AI

We use a few web tools in class, including ClassroomStreet.com, a financial simulation I built. You log in with a class number and a PIN. No personal information goes into it.

You may use AI tools to brainstorm, check your work, or translate. You may not use them to do a project for you, and in this class most of the work is something you cook, sew, build, or say out loud, so it would not help anyway. If you use AI for a written piece, say so at the bottom.

## Cost

## A note for families

FACS is the class where your child learns to cook, sew, budget, and plan. Ask them what they made this week. If you have a skill you would share (a recipe, a craft, a job you could talk about for ten minutes), email me. I would love to have you in.
