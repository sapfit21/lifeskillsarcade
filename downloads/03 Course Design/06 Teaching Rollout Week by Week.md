# Teaching Rollout, Week by Week

The full course as 36 teaching weeks of five meetings each, against a standard school year, no dates. A holiday pushes everything one meeting to the right and nothing else changes. Marking periods close near days 45, 90, 135, and 180. Every week that contains a lab, a machine day, or a project build is flagged, because those are the weeks that punish poor preparation.

Three standing rules that make every flagged week survivable. Food labs are shopped the weekend before, never the morning of, and the allergy check goes home a full week before the first lab that needs it. Machines are tested with thread on scrap before the machine block, never during it. Nothing is cut, cooked, or sewn on a day the safety routine has not already been taught.

The flex versions (30 weeks, one semester, every other day, two year spiral) are in `01 Scope and Sequence.md`; this rollout is the 36-week daily plan.

## The weeks that punish poor prep, in one list

Lab weeks: 4, 5, 7, 21, 31, 33. Machine weeks: 26 and 27, with the machine test the standing rule for week 24. Sewing weeks: 25 and 28. Project build weeks: 18, 22, 23, 28, 32, 33, 35, 36. The single highest-stakes prep item of the year is the week 24 machine test, because six teaching days ride on the machines working, and the second is the week 33 shop for Feed the Class, because the class wrote the list and the class is watching whether their plan gets honored.

## Grade entry weeks

Marking periods close near days 45 (week 9), 90 (week 18), 135 (week 27), and 180 (week 36). Each closes on a week that also carries an assessment, so enter grades the same weekend and do not let them stack.
