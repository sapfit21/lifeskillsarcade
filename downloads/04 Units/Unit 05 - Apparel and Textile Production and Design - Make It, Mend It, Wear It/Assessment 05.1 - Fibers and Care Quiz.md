# Assessment 5.1: Fibers and Care Quiz

**Unit:** Unit 5, Make It, Mend It, Wear It (Apparel and Textile Production and Design)
**Topic:** 5.1 What We Wear and Why (Lessons 5.1 to 5.4)
**When:** The last 15 minutes of Lesson 5.4 Day 2 (day 120), starting at minute 25 of the period. Step 4 of that lesson's procedure holds the slot.
**Category:** Topic assessments (25 percent). One retake after a review; best score counts (see the retake note at the end).
**Points:** 24
**Allowed:** a pencil, and a calculator on item 12. The care symbol chart comes down off the wall before the quiz. Handouts, folders, and notes go away.
**Time:** 15 minutes. Extended time: finish in the first five minutes of Lesson 5.5, where the do now is a tool naming task and nothing is missed.

This is the formal check for Topic 5.1. Every care symbol on this quiz is described in words as well as drawn, so no item depends on how well a symbol prints or on reading English alone.

## Retake rule

One retake, after a review, and the best score counts, per the Grading Plan. The retake is a different form of the same twelve items (different fibers in Part A, different symbols in item 6, a different ruined garment, and the same cost math with different numbers) and it runs during the first ten minutes of Lesson 5.6 or at a lunch period in the same week. No student is kept out of the sewing work because of this score.

## Missed-the-day version

A student absent on day 120 takes the quiz in the first fifteen minutes of the next period they attend, at a side table, while the class runs the do now and Step 1. If they missed Lesson 5.3 as well, they run the six-swatch hand test at the table first, which takes about six minutes, and then take the quiz, because Part A is not a memory item, it is a test of something they were supposed to have touched.
