# Calendar 06: From Farm to Table

Unit 6, Food Systems and Production. 24 periods of 40 minutes, days 144 to 167 of the year, all inside marking period 4. Five teaching weeks; the last week is four meetings. One cell per period: the lesson code and title in bold on the first line, then what happens that day. **LAB** marks a period where students cook and are scored on `Rubric 01 - Lab Rubric.md`. **CHECK** marks a formal assessment. **PROJECT** marks a Feed the Class project day.

This calendar is written in day numbers and teaching weeks, not dates. The columns are the five meetings of each teaching week; a holiday pushes every later day back one meeting. Map the day numbers onto your school's calendar; the day numbers and the order do not change.

## Counts

- **Lessons:** 18 (6.1 to 6.18). Two-day lessons: 6.5, 6.7, 6.10, 6.13, 6.16, 6.18.
- **LAB days: 5** (days 152, 153, 154, 163, 164). Days 152, 153, and 154 are the three Topic 6.2 labs, each scored on `Rubric 01 - Lab Rubric.md` (16 points). Days 163 and 164 are the two Feed the Class cook days, scored on the same lab rubric for the individual lab score and on `Rubric 06` criterion 4 for the project. Labs 3 and 4 of the year are days 153 and 154; the quick bread lab on day 152 and the cook days on days 163 and 164 are scored on the lab rubric but are not numbered labs.
- **CHECK days: 4** (days 149, 157, 165, 167). Day 149: the Food Systems Quiz closes Topic 6.1 in the last 15 minutes of Lesson 6.5 Day 2. Day 157: the Cooking Methods Check closes Topic 6.2 in the last 15 minutes of Lesson 6.11. Day 165: the project folder comes in and is scored on Rubric 06, which is Topic 6.3's check. Day 167: the unit test in the last 20 minutes, which also covers Topic 6.4.
- **PROJECT days: 8** (days 158 to 165). Three of those days end in a required teacher signature: the costing sheet on day 160, the station card on day 161, and the consolidated shopping list on day 162. No signature, no next step.
- **Topic day counts:** 6.1 six (days 144 to 149), 6.2 eight (days 150 to 157), 6.3 eight (days 158 to 165), 6.4 two (days 166 to 167). Total 24.
- **Marking period:** all 24 days are inside MP4 (days 136 to 180).
- **Shopping day:** the teacher shops between day 162 and day 163, from the approved list, and brings the receipt to day 165. Buy the day before cook day, not the same morning, so cold items are cold and a substitution can be made calmly if something is out.

## If a day is lost

A snow day, an assembly, a field trip, or a state test pushes everything one cell to the right. Drop days in this order to get back on schedule:

1. **6.10 Day 2** (day 156). Fold the presentations into Day 1 as a two-minute gallery share. The research card is still written and still scored.
2. **6.2** (day 145). Fold local and seasonal into Lesson 6.1's debrief and send the New York seasonal chart home as this unit's one homework sheet.
3. **6.5 Day 2** (day 149). Do the packaging redesign as a sketch instead of a prototype, and move the Food Systems Quiz to the last 15 minutes of Lesson 6.4 on day 147.
4. **6.7 Day 1** (day 151). Run the acid and base fizz test as a five-minute teacher demonstration at the start of Day 2 and give the hypothesis sheet as that day's do now. This one costs the most in learning of the four, so it is last.

**Never drop these:** 6.14 (day 161, the flowchart; without it cook day fails and the reflection has nothing to measure against), 6.15 (day 162, the approval checklist; without it there is no food to cook), 6.16 (days 163 and 164, cook day itself), and 6.4 (day 147, food deserts; it is the lesson that answers why any of this matters to a student who is not going to be a chef).

**If a day is lost inside the project (days 158 to 165):** the three signature checkpoints hold their order no matter what. The costing sheet is signed before the flowchart is built, the station card is approved before the shopping list is consolidated, and the list is approved before anybody shops. If the loss lands between day 162 and day 163, move the shopping trip, not the checkpoints. **Do not run cook day on an unapproved list or an unapproved station card.** If only one cook day survives, run all three or four stations at once on that one day with the class eating in the last ten minutes, drop the final course, and fold the Lesson 6.17 service into the same period as a shortened setting of 30 covers; the reflection then moves to the do now and first fifteen minutes of day 166, and the careers content compresses into one day.

**If a day is lost at the end:** the unit test moves to the do now and first twenty minutes of Unit 7 Lesson 7.1 on day 168, and the careers self-check is collected as the exit card on day 166. Do not shorten the test from 20 minutes.

**If the loss is a whole week** (a school closure, a testing week): run the 21-day version in the unit plan's Section 6, which drops flex lessons 1, 2, and 3 above and leaves the project untouched. The project is the thing a principal will ask about; protect its eight days before anything else.
