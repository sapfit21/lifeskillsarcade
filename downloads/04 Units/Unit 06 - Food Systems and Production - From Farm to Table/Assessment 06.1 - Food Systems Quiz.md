## How to use

- Item 7 is the one most students lose. The two-step division (by students, then by days) is the whole difficulty. If more than a third of the class misses it, that is a math reteach, not a content reteach, and it takes three minutes on the board.
- Items 6 and 12 are both trade-off items with no single right answer. Score the reasoning, not the position. A student who picks the answer you disagree with and defends it on both sides gets full credit.
- Item 10 is the one to read before you hand the quizzes back. A student answer that reveals something personal about their own household gets a private conversation, not a comment in the margin, and see the three rules in Lesson 6.4.
- Retake version: change the numbers in Part C, swap the six-step chain in item 1 to milk, and keep everything else. The retake is the same test with new numbers, not an easier one.
