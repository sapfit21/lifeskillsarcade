## How to use

- Items 2 to 5 are the heart of this check. If a student names a method with a real reason, the point is earned even if the key lists another method. Broccoli sautéed with a reason about fast heat and color is as good an answer as steamed.
- Item 6 is the item that separates students who watched the demonstration from students who did not. It is worth reading these first.
- Items 7 to 10 are science method items. If a whole class misses item 8, the problem is the word "constant," not the lab, and it is a thirty-second fix.
- Item 12 must not turn into naming a classmate. The directions say "name the mistake, not a person" on purpose. Any answer that names a student by name gets the point if the mistake is correct, and gets a quiet word about the wording.
- Retake version: swap the eight methods in item 1 for the other order, change the four foods in Part B (rice, a chicken breast, an egg, an apple), keep Part D as written because the class ran that experiment, and rewrite the Part F scenario as a pasta lab where the water was never turned on until minute 8. Same difficulty, different surface.
- Before the retake, the review is five minutes: the two heat columns on the board, the four batch table, and the place setting cut-outs from Lesson 6.11.
