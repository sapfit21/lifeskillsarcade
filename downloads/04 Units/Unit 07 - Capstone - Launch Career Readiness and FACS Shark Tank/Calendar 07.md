# Calendar 07: Launch: Career Readiness and FACS Shark Tank

Unit 7, the capstone. 13 periods of 40 minutes, days 168 to 180 of the year, all inside marking period 4. Two full teaching weeks plus three meetings. One cell per period: the lesson code and title in bold on the first line, then what happens that day. **CHECK** marks a formal assessment on `Rubric 07`. **PROJECT** marks a FACS Shark Tank project day. **GATE** marks a day that ends in a required teacher signature; no signature, no next step.

This calendar is written in day numbers and teaching weeks, not dates. The columns are the five meetings of each teaching week; a holiday pushes every later day back one meeting. Map the day numbers onto your school's calendar; the day numbers and the order do not change.

## Counts

- **Lessons:** 12 (7.1 to 7.12). Two-day lessons: 7.10 only, in this calendar. **Lesson 7.8 is written for two days and runs as one here;** see the note in its cell and in the unit plan's Section 6.
- **CHECK days: 4** (days 172, 178, 179, and 180). Day 172: `Rubric 07` criteria 1 and 2, 8 points, in the last 15 minutes of Lesson 7.5, which is the whole of Topic 7.1's formal check. Day 178: criteria 3, 4, and 5, 12 points, scored from the team folder and the prototype handed in at the end of the period. Day 179: criterion 6, 4 points, scored live during the twenty-second swaps between pitches. Day 180: the year reflection, which with `Rubric 07` is the course final, plus the portfolio checklist scored complete, partial, or missing.
- **PROJECT days: 7** (days 173 to 179). Two of those days end in a required teacher signature: the filter sheet on day 174 and the planner sheet plus the signed numbers page on day 177. No signature, no next step.
- **GATE days: 2** (days 174 and 177).
- **Topic day counts:** 7.1 five (days 168 to 172), 7.2 eight (days 173 to 180). Total 13.
- **Marking period:** all 13 days are inside MP4 (days 136 to 180). Every score in this unit lands in MP4, which is why criteria 1 and 2 are scored the night of day 172, criteria 3 to 5 the night of day 178, and criterion 6 live on day 179. Nothing is left to score on day 180 except the reflection.
- **LAB days: 0.** This is the only unit in the course with no lab and no `Rubric 01` score. Say so in the gradebook note so a parent looking at MP4 does not wonder where the lab grades went; MP4 already holds Unit 5's and Unit 6's.
- **Guest day: 1** (day 179). Confirm the panel in September, send the briefing on day 177, set the judge table before the bell on day 179.
- **Consumables:** one poster board per team on day 177 and one labeled folder per student on day 180. About $25 to $35 a section. This is the cheapest unit in the course.
- **The object that leaves the building:** the Career Readiness portfolio, on day 180, in a student's hand. Photograph the resume, the reflection, and the checklist before it goes.

## If a day is lost

An assembly, a field trip, a state test, a fire drill, a half day, or a June schedule that ends early pushes everything one cell to the right. **A short June is normal, not an emergency**, and this unit is the one most likely to get squeezed because it sits at the very end of the year. Drop days in this order:

1. **7.3, ACT FAST** (day 170). Fold the four traits into Lesson 7.4's anchor chart as a five-minute block: after the class names what a boss looks for, sort their own words into the four traits and hand out the self-score sheet as that day's exit card. The scenario deck becomes an optional five-minute warm-up on any later day. **What it costs:** the best day in Topic 7.1 for classroom climate, and the drill that makes a student unafraid of an unplanned judge question. **What it does not cost:** any rubric criterion. That is why it is first.
2. **7.6, What a Business Is** (day 173). Fold the four questions and the profit example into Lesson 7.7's do now and first block, using two businesses instead of five and the ten-shirt version of the T-shirt example instead of forty. **What it costs:** the revenue, cost, and profit teaching that Lesson 7.8's numbers page stands on. Only drop this if Unit 2's money work was strong and you can name which lesson taught profit.
3. **7.10 Day 2** (day 178). Rehearse twice inside Day 1's last twelve minutes and run the peer review as a written swap of the peer review sheet rather than a delivered pitch. Score criteria 3, 4, and 5 from the folder collected at the end of Day 1. **What it costs:** pitch quality, visibly, in front of guests. A team that has not rehearsed standing three times runs long and gets cut off before the ask.
4. **7.2, Build Your Resume** (day 169), and only if students have devices and can draft outside class. **What it costs:** the two-minute conference with every student, which is the single most valuable fifteen minutes in Topic 7.1 and the reason more than eighty-seven of my students finished a resume instead of starting one. Last for a reason.

**Never drop these:** **7.1** (without the folder mining, the resume is invented and the entire capstone argument collapses), **7.5** (the only formal check in Topic 7.1, and the interview is half of what a principal is being shown), **7.7** (the gate that decides whether the project is finishable in the days left), **7.11** (pitch day itself), and **7.12** (the portfolio and the year reflection, which is the course final under the grading plan).

**If a day is lost inside the project (days 173 to 179):** the two signature gates hold their order no matter what. The filter sheet is signed before any customer is interviewed, and the planner sheet and numbers page are signed before anything goes on a poster. If the loss lands between day 177 and day 178, move the rehearsal, not the gates. **Do not run pitch day with unsigned numbers pages;** a team pitching arithmetic nobody checked in front of an administrator is the one failure mode in this unit that is genuinely embarrassing, and it is entirely preventable by a ten-second signature.

**If only one build day survives:** run day 177's Numbers Clinic in the first 12 minutes, the planner and panels in 16, the prototype in 8, and one timed rehearsal in 4. Skip the peer review and take the two revisions from the teacher's own walk instead. Score criteria 3, 4, and 5 from the folder collected at the bell.

**If pitch day is the day that gets cut:** do not cancel it, compress it. Run it in the last 25 minutes of day 180 with the teacher plus three student judges, 45 seconds per pitch and 45 seconds of questions, and move the portfolio and reflection to the first 15 minutes of the same period with the reflection cut to prompts 1 and 4. Criterion 6 is still scored. A capstone with no pitch day is not a shorter capstone, it is a different unit.

**If a whole week is lost** (a closure, a testing week, an early last day): run the **5-day version in the unit plan's Section 6**, which is Lessons 7.1 and 7.2 combined, 7.4 with 7.3's reveal folded in, 7.5 with the check, 7.7 with 7.6 and 7.8 compressed, and one combined pitch period. `Rubric 07` is then scored on criteria 1, 2, 3, and 6 only, out of 16, and the portfolio and reflection move to the last day of Unit 6. Say so in the syllabus, because IGLR 3. d) and 5. b) still need a home.

**If the year gives you fourteen periods instead of thirteen,** which happens when the calendar's last day lands later than the sample, spend the extra period on **Lesson 7.8 Day 2**: a full class period on unit cost, margin, break-even, and the projection, with the peer number check at the end. Everything after it shifts one cell right and pitch day moves to day 180 with the reflection on day 181. That is the better version of this unit and it is the one to ask for, because unit cost and break-even are the most transferable arithmetic anywhere in the course.
