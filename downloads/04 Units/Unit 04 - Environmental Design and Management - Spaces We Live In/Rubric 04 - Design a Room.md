## How to use this rubric

- Hand the rubric out with the project sheet in Lesson 4.11 so students design against it. Nothing on it should be a surprise.
- The student or team fills the self-assessment row at the end of Lesson 4.15, before the teacher scores. Honest self-assessment is one of the Exceeds conditions on criterion 5.
- Criterion 1 is scored from the Needs and Wants List (Lesson 4.11) and checked against the plan and the model: were the needs actually met?
- Criterion 2 is scored on the revised plan after the Lesson 4.12 Day 2 peer review, not the first draft. The revision note on the back counts.
- Criterion 3 is scored from the Budget Sheet as signed in Lesson 4.13. Recompute the tax line on every sheet; that is where the errors hide.
- Criterion 4 is scored on the clipboard in Lesson 4.14 Day 3 (matches the plan, scheme named, labels give a reason, scale written, justification has a need and a principle) and confirmed at the gallery walk. The isometric drawing alternative and a digital model are scored on the same descriptors.
- Criterion 5 is scored live in Lesson 4.15 with the five-part clipboard checklist. A student who pitched to the teacher alone at the station is scored the same way.
- Every descriptor is written as what the student did. If the work does not match any box, pick the closest and write why on the notes line.
- A team of two gets one score on criteria 1 to 4. On criterion 5, note on the notes line if one teammate did not speak; the grading plan's participation category handles the rest.
- A late project is accepted with a one-level drop on one criterion, never a zero, per the grading plan.
- The rubric goes home with the model after the Unit 4 Test. Students keep the self-assessment in the FACS folder.
