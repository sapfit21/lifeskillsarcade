## Part 1: Label parts (5 points)

**1.** Match each label term to what it tells you. Write the letter on the line.

| Term | | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ___ Serving size | | A. How much of a full day's amount one serving gives you, based on 2,000 calories |
| ___ Servings per container | | B. Sugar the company put in, not sugar that was already in the food |
| ___ Percent daily value | | C. The amount of food that all the numbers on the label describe |
| ___ Added sugars | | D. How many servings are in the whole package |
| ___ Dietary fiber | | E. A nutrient to get more of; it helps digestion and keeps you full |

**Scoring notes**
- 24 to 30: got it. 18 to 23: review the items missed and offer the retake. Under 18: retake after a one-on-one review with the Spot the Trick handout.
- Item 12 is the one to read carefully. A student who picks B with no numbers has the opinion, not the skill; that is a 1 of 6.
- Items 8, 9, and 10 are the percent daily value and multiplication skills that the $40 project math check (Lesson 1.19) builds on. Note who missed them.

**ELL and IEP versions**
- ELL: the two labels stay the same; items 1 and 2 include the word bank from `Vocabulary 01.md` in Turkish, Portuguese, and Spanish; items 11 and 12 may be answered in short phrases with the sentence frames "Source ___ is credible because ___" and "Label ___ is better because ___ has ___ and ___ has ___."
- IEP and 504: extended time; the quiz may be read aloud; items 11 and 12 may be answered orally and recorded by the teacher; a calculator is already allowed for everyone.
- Grade 6 support: give items 1 through 10 and item 12 without sentence 3 (25 points; scale to 30).
- Grade 8 stretch: add one item: "Label B's fiber is 4 g. The daily value for fiber is 28 g. Compute the percent daily value and check it against the label." (4 divided by 28 = 0.143, about 14%; the label says 14%.) Bonus 2 points.
