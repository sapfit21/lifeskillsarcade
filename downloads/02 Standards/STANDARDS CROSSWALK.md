## PART A. How to cite

Use these five formats on every lesson, project, and rubric. Copy the line text exactly as printed in the state document, including the state's own typos (see Part D and Part E).

**A.1 NYS FACS content module line.** Module short code, topic number and name, letter, line text.
NYS N&W 1. Culinary Management b) Demonstrate safety and sanitation procedures when handling food and equipment

**A.2 CTE theme module line.** Same shape, with "CTE" in front of the theme code.
CTE HSW 4. Classroom and Workplace Safety a) List and describe the purpose of safety rules specific to classroom and workplace settings
For the Problem Solving and Innovation theme, which nests a second level, add the sub-letter after the letter:
CTE PSI 2. Design Process (Proactive) a) f. Developing and constructing a prototype or model of the selected design

**A.3 NYS FACS Learning Standard (Intermediate).** Standard number and name, "(Intermediate)", the key idea, then the performance indicator. Each of the three standards has exactly one key idea at the Intermediate level, so "Key Idea" always means that one.
NYS FACS Standard 2 (Intermediate), Key Idea: Students will know the basic principles of home and community safety. They can demonstrate the skills necessary to maintain their homes and workplaces in a safe and comfortable condition. They can provide a safe and nurturing environment for themselves and others.; PI: Students know the basics of managing a safe and healthy home.
The PI letters (A, B, C) used in the tables below are NYLearns codes, not NYSED's own; the 1996 print document lists the indicators as bullets. Keep the letters for internal tracking and quote the PI text on the lesson.

**A.4 CDOS (Intermediate).** Standard number and name; for 3a add the sub skill.
CDOS 1 Career Development
CDOS 2 Integrated Learning
CDOS 3a Universal Foundation Skills: Managing Resources

**A.5 National FCS.** "National FCS 3.0:" then the competency number and a faithful paraphrase of the competency. Paraphrased on 2026-09-14 (D23): LEAD FCS Education's terms allow verbatim reproduction only for non-commercial use, so the build cites the number exactly and restates the descriptor. The verbatim text stays in `02 Standards/NYS ML CTE FACS Modules - verbatim extract of all twelve.md` and the web research file for checking.
National FCS 3.0: 14.3.3 Show the ability to select, store, prepare, and serve food that is nourishing and looks good on the plate.

**Why 3.0 and not "2nd ed."** The project brief says "National Standards for FCS Education, 2nd ed." The second edition (2008 to 2018) is superseded. The current edition is "Family and Consumer Sciences National Standards 3.0," copyright 2018 to 2028, developed by LEAD FCS Education (formerly NASAFACS). Most numbers carry over between editions but wording changed, and the NYS Nutrition and Wellness module PDF itself prints the older wording for many 14.x lines (see Part E.1). So: cite 3.0 numbers, quote 3.0 wording from the LEAD FCS compilation PDF, and credit LEAD FCS Education visibly on the site (a condition of the non-commercial terms of use). Area 7 is "Family and Human Services" in 3.0, not "Family and Community Services."

### A.8 How the Part B tables were filled

- **Line and Line text:** verbatim from standards_extract.md, including the state's typos.
- **Proposed unit:** one primary unit, others in parentheses.
- **CTE theme lines:** one or two lines from the theme module text, cited by code. Every code here exists in standards_extract.md; none were invented.
- **NYS FACS standard:** chosen only from the standards the module's own Standards Addressed block lists (IGLR: 2, 3; HDR: 2; ATPD: 2; EDM: 2, 3; N&W: 1, 2, 3; FSP: 1, 2, 3). Where a better fit exists outside the block, Part E.3 says so.
- **CDOS:** 1, 2, or 3a with sub skill. Every module cites all three.
- **National FCS 3.0:** only numbers that (a) appear in the module's own Standards Addressed block or (b) are confirmed verbatim in external_standards.md. Text is paraphrased from the 3.0 compilation (see A.5) as given in external_standards.md. "(block)" means the number is printed in that module's block; "(ext)" means it is confirmed in external_standards.md but not printed in the module block. Where the module block prints older wording, the note says "block wording differs."
