# Slides 0.1: What Is FACS and Who Is in This Room

Lesson 0.1, Unit 0. One period. Ten slides.

## Slide 1: Do now

- Take an index card from the door. Sit at the table with your number.
- On the card: your first name, and one thing you already know how to do that a family needs.
- Cook one thing, watch a little kid, fix something, find a deal, sew a button, anything.
- One line. Do not talk yet.

Notes: The board says this before anyone walks in. Every day starts this way. Cold call four cards; write the four skills on the board.

## Slide 2: Six objects, six units

- Everything on this board fits under one of six units.
- By June you will have done all six.
- Watch the tray.

Image: a towel being lifted off a tray with a wooden spoon, a coin, a baby sock, a paint chip, a needle and thread, and a can of beans.

Notes: Pull the towel. Hold up each object as you name the unit on the next slide.

## Slide 3: The year map

- The spoon: Kitchen Skills and Smart Eating. You cook real labs.
- The coin: Me, My Goals, My Money. You run a stock market simulation.
- The sock: Growing Up, Getting Along. You plan a babysitting kit.
- The paint chip: Spaces We Live In. You design a room.
- The needle: Make It, Mend It, Wear It. You sew something you keep.
- The can: From Farm to Table. You trace a food from the farm to the plate.

Notes: Six units, about five to six weeks each. Say it once: the first cooking lab is on day 16. Students fill Part 1 of the handout.

## Slide 4: What you already do

- Every skill on the board is already in one of the six.
- Find yours. Write it in the home column.
- "Nothing yet" is allowed.
- Star two units where you already have a skill.

Notes: Model with one do now card: "eggs" goes under Kitchen Skills. Six minutes.

## Slide 5: Who is at this table

- Go around. Say your name, your skill from the card, and one thing you can offer this table this year.
- "I am calm." "I am fast at math." "I can translate." "I am not afraid to talk first."
- The recorder writes the four offers on the table card.
- Sentence starters: My name is ___. I can ___. I can offer ___.

Notes: Six minutes. Walk and listen. Note the tables where one person does all the talking; that matters on day 3.

## Slide 6: What you can count on

- I will tell you the day before if anything hot or sharp is coming.
- The board will tell you what to do before you sit. Every day.
- When the lights blink and I count down, hands go empty and eyes come here. You practice that tomorrow.

Notes: The line behind this slide, said out loud: "Kids do not learn from people they do not trust. So here is what you can trust."

## Slide 7: Two more things

- Mondays start with a quote on the board and one sentence about it.
- Nothing in the first five days is graded.

Image: a calendar with Monday circled.

## Slide 8: The hope card

- The unit I hope to reach first is ___.
- Why (two sentences).
- One thing I can offer my table this year.
- Tear it off and hand it in.

Notes: Four minutes. Grade 6: one sentence. Grade 8: also the unit you think you will like least and what could change your mind.

## Slide 9: The count

- Three people read their unit and one sentence.
- Hands up by unit. I write the six numbers on the board.
- They stay up all week.

Notes: The count is tomorrow's do now opener.

## Slide 10: Tomorrow

- Tomorrow: the room tour. Where everything is, and the reset cue.
- And a game where you describe a picture and someone else has to draw it.
- Bring nothing. The board will tell you what to do.
