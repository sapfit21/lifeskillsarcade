# Slides 0.5: The Room and Lab Agreement and Pre-Assessment

Lesson 0.5, Unit 0. One period. Eleven slides.

## Slide 1: Do now

- Four days in. Write one rule for this room you already know without being told.
- Then write one rule you think this room needs that nobody has said yet.
- One line each.

Notes: Three of each on the board. Every one of them is in the agreement or about to be.

## Slide 2: Why this paper

- On day 16 this kitchen opens for anyone who signed.
- Nobody cooks who did not.
- You read it, you change what can be changed, you sign it. So do I.

Image: a single sheet with three signature lines.

## Slide 3: Read it in parts

- Eight sections. One per table.
- Read your section aloud, one student per line.
- After each section, someone from a different table says it in their own words.
- Underline any line you do not understand or do not agree with.

Notes: Nine minutes. Sections: 1 safety, 2 respect, 3 roles, 4 clean up, 5 phones, 6 food and allergies, 7 the reset cue, 8 what happens when it is broken.

## Slide 4: The allergy line, for real

- The nurse's list for this room this year, read aloud
- Not for a lab. Not for a snack. Not for a birthday.
- Anything you cannot eat: tell me before the first lab, privately if you want. The lab will have a version you can eat.

Notes: Read this against the real list. A general line does nothing on lab day.

## Slide 5: Two sections are not up for a vote

- Section 1, safety. Section 6, food and allergies.
- Everything else, we wrote together, and you can change it today.
- Why those two: they are the two things that can send someone to the hospital.

## Slide 6: Compare and rewrite

- Look at the team norms chart from yesterday.
- One norm the agreement already covers. One it does not.
- Pick one line your table would change: clearer, stricter, or fairer.
- Frame: Change "___" to "___" because ___.

Notes: Nine minutes including the vote. One rewrite per table on chart paper.

## Slide 7: Vote

- Each table reads its rewrite.
- Hands up to add it.
- The ones that pass go in section 9, "Rules we added," on every copy.

Notes: If a rewrite loosens section 1 or 6, let it go to the vote, then explain why those two are fixed.

## Slide 8: Sign

- Your signature means: I read it, I helped write part of it, I will keep it.
- I sign it too. It binds me.
- The family line is optional. It lets someone at home see how this room runs.
- Sign both copies. One in the tray, one goes home.

Notes: Sign the teacher line on the class copy in front of them. Three minutes.

## Slide 9: The pre-test

- This is not graded.
- It tells me what Unit 1 can skip.
- Write what you think. A blank tells me nothing. A wrong guess tells me a lot.
- Twelve minutes.

Notes: Hand it out face down. Start the timer. Collect at the bell.

## Slide 10: Exit card

- One line of the agreement I will find hardest to keep, and why.
- The rule we added that I am gladdest is in there.
- Two people read line two aloud.

## Slide 11: Monday

- Monday is Unit 1. Hunt the Hazard.
- There will be twelve things in this room that could hurt someone.
- I put them there. You find them.
