## Teacher key

There is no single right plan. A plan is signed when:
- every line in Part 2 has a size, a price, and a unit, and the cost equals the price times the number to buy;
- the running total is correct line by line and the final total is at or under $40;
- pantry items from the card are marked $0;
- Part 3 shows at least four groups with a real dish in each, and every rule on the card is checked with a dish named;
- Part 4 has one sentence per change;
- the station dish in Part 5 passes the five rules and totals $20 or under.

Usual errors: cost written as the recipe amount instead of the package (a 16 oz need priced as one 8 oz bag); a unit missing so the quantity is wrong; a drink counted as dairy; a rule on the card checked only for allergies.
