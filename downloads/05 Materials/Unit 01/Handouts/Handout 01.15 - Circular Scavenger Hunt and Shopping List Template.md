## Part 4: The wait decision

Circle two items on your list that should wait for a better week. For each one, write the reason using a price or a date.

1. Wait on ____________ because ________________________________ (price or date: ____________)

## Teacher key

Part 1: 1. $1.99; 2. the 8 oz bag; 3. limit 4; 4. Friday to Thursday; 5. $1.99 with card, $2.99 without.

Part 2: answers depend on the circular. A "got it" hunt finds eight of ten with a unit on every price. Row 5 math: half the single price if two are taken; a deal only if the family will use two before it spoils.

Part 3: answers depend on the recipe. Check that (a) pantry items from the board are crossed off and not on the list, (b) every item has a quantity, (c) every item is in a sensible section (shredded cheese in dairy, tortillas in dry goods or the bread aisle, pre-cooked chicken in meat and deli, frozen corn in frozen), (d) sale prices carry a unit.

Part 4: any two items with a reason that names a price (not on sale, cheaper next week per the circular) or a date (the sale starts after the shopping day, the item is not needed until later). "Because we do not like it" is not a wait reason; it is a plan change.
