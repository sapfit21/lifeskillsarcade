# Handout 02.14: Investor Journal

Name: ______________________ Period: ______ Merchant number: ______ Start date: __________

This journal stays in your FACS folder for the whole ClassroomStreet Investor Challenge. You write one entry every class day. Every entry has three parts and one number:

- **What I did** (bought, sold, or held; which venture; how many units)
- **Why I did it** (a dispatch, a risk level, my pie, a feeling you noticed)
- **What happened** (did the price move, did my fortune move)
- **My total fortune** at the bell (the number on the screen)

Three sentences and the number is a complete entry. More is welcome.

Sentence starters (Turkish, Portuguese, and Spanish lines are marked [check with a native speaker]):
