# Handout 2.21: Career Cluster Walk Posters and Match Sheet

Two parts. Part A is the sixteen posters: print each section on one sheet of card stock, one poster per page, and tape them around the room in number order before class. Print the eight posters marked FACS-RELATED on a different color. Part B is the match sheet, one per student.

Interest letters use the six letters from the Lesson 2.2 interest quiz (each student has two): R (Realistic: builds, fixes, works with hands and tools), I (Investigative: studies, solves, asks why), A (Artistic: creates, designs, performs), S (Social: helps, teaches, cares for people), E (Enterprising: leads, sells, persuades, starts things), C (Conventional: organizes, tracks, follows systems, works with data).

* * *

# Part A: The sixteen posters

## Poster 1: Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: growing, raising, catching, and processing food and plants; caring for animals; managing land, water, and forests.

Three jobs:
- Veterinarian (cares for animals; a doctor's degree)
- Food scientist (tests and improves the food in packages; a four-year degree)
- Farm or greenhouse manager (runs the growing; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: R, I

Image: a row of green plants in a field with a barn behind them.

## Poster 2: Architecture and Construction

What this cluster is: designing, building, fixing, and maintaining buildings, roads, and bridges.

Three jobs:
- Electrician (wires buildings; apprenticeship)
- Architect (designs buildings; a four-year degree plus more)
- Carpenter (builds with wood; apprenticeship or on-the-job training)

Interest letters that often fit: R, C, I

Image: a house frame under construction with a hard hat on a beam.

## Poster 3: Arts, A/V Technology and Communications (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: creating, designing, performing, writing, filming, and publishing.

Three jobs:
- Graphic designer (designs logos, menus, packages; a two-year or four-year degree)
- Video editor (cuts and builds videos; a certificate or on-the-job training)
- Fashion designer (designs clothing; a two-year or four-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: A, E

Image: a camera, a paintbrush, and a microphone in a row.

## Poster 4: Business Management and Administration (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: planning, organizing, and running a business or an office so it works.

Three jobs:
- Restaurant or store manager (runs the place and the staff; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)
- Human resources specialist (hires and supports workers; a four-year degree)
- Office administrator (keeps the schedule, the files, and the supplies; a certificate or on-the-job training)

Interest letters that often fit: E, C

Image: a desk with a laptop, a calendar, and a coffee cup.

## Poster 5: Education and Training (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: teaching, coaching, and helping people of every age learn.

Three jobs:
- Teacher (a four-year degree plus a state certificate)
- Preschool teacher (a two-year degree or a certificate)
- Athletic coach or fitness instructor (a certificate; some jobs need a degree)

Interest letters that often fit: S, A

Image: a whiteboard with a lesson on it and a hand raised.

## Poster 6: Finance

What this cluster is: managing money for people and businesses: banking, insurance, investing, and taxes.

Three jobs:
- Bank teller (handles deposits and questions; on-the-job training)
- Accountant (keeps and checks the books; a four-year degree)
- Financial advisor (helps people plan and invest; a four-year degree plus licenses)

Interest letters that often fit: C, E

Image: a calculator on top of a bank statement.

## Poster 7: Government and Public Administration

What this cluster is: running towns, states, and the country: planning, records, elections, and services.

Three jobs:
- City planner (decides where parks, roads, and buildings go; a four-year degree plus more)
- Postal worker (sorts and delivers mail; on-the-job training)
- Town clerk (keeps records, licenses, and meeting notes; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: E, S, C

Image: a town hall with a flag in front.

## Poster 8: Health Science (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: caring for people's health: treating, testing, and preventing illness.

Three jobs:
- Registered nurse (a two-year or four-year degree plus a license)
- Dental hygienist (cleans teeth and checks gums; a two-year degree plus a license)
- Registered dietitian (plans food for health; a four-year degree plus more and a license)

Interest letters that often fit: I, S

Image: a stethoscope and a clipboard.

## Poster 9: Hospitality and Tourism (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: restaurants, hotels, travel, events, and recreation: taking care of guests.

Three jobs:
- Chef (runs a kitchen; a certificate, culinary school, or years of on-the-job training)
- Hotel front desk manager (runs check-in and the guest experience; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)
- Event planner (plans weddings, parties, and conferences; on-the-job training or a four-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: E, S, R

Image: a chef's hat next to a hotel key card.

## Poster 10: Human Services (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: helping people and families with their needs: counseling, child care, elder care, community programs.

Three jobs:
- Child care director (runs a day care or preschool center; a two-year or four-year degree)
- Social worker (helps families get what they need; a four-year degree plus more)
- Home health aide (cares for people at home; a certificate)

Interest letters that often fit: S, E

Image: two hands, one large and one small, holding.

## Poster 11: Information Technology

What this cluster is: computers, networks, apps, and keeping data safe.

Three jobs:
- Software developer (builds apps and programs; a four-year degree, or a certificate plus a strong portfolio)
- IT support specialist (fixes computer problems for a company; a certificate or a two-year degree)
- Cybersecurity analyst (protects systems from attacks; a four-year degree or certifications)

Interest letters that often fit: I, C, R

Image: a laptop with a lock on the screen.

## Poster 12: Law, Public Safety, Corrections and Security

What this cluster is: keeping people safe and the law working: police, fire, courts, and security.

Three jobs:
- Firefighter (a certificate and an academy; some need a two-year degree)
- Paralegal (helps lawyers prepare cases; a two-year degree or a certificate)
- Police officer (a police academy; some departments need college credits)

Interest letters that often fit: R, S, E

Image: a fire helmet and a set of scales.

## Poster 13: Manufacturing

What this cluster is: making things in factories and shops, from cars to cereal, and checking that they are made right.

Three jobs:
- Machinist (cuts and shapes metal parts; apprenticeship or a certificate)
- Quality control inspector (checks that products meet the standard; on-the-job training)
- Food production supervisor (runs a line in a food plant; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: R, C

Image: a factory line with boxes moving on a belt.

## Poster 14: Marketing (FACS-RELATED)

What this cluster is: getting people to know about and buy products and services: ads, brands, sales, and social media.

Three jobs:
- Social media manager (runs a brand's accounts; a four-year degree or a strong portfolio)
- Retail sales associate (helps customers and sells; on-the-job training)
- Market researcher (studies what customers want; a four-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: E, A

Image: a phone screen with a store's post and a shopping bag.

## Poster 15: Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics

What this cluster is: researching, testing, designing, and calculating: the science and engineering behind everything else.

Three jobs:
- Mechanical engineer (designs machines; a four-year degree)
- Lab technician (runs tests in a lab; a two-year degree or a certificate)
- Statistician (works with numbers and data; a four-year degree plus more)

Interest letters that often fit: I, R

Image: a beaker, a gear, and a graph.

## Poster 16: Transportation, Distribution and Logistics

What this cluster is: moving people and things by road, rail, air, and sea, and running the warehouses in between.

Three jobs:
- Truck driver (a commercial driver's license; a few weeks of training)
- Airline pilot (flight school and licenses; many need a four-year degree)
- Warehouse or logistics manager (runs shipping and storage; on-the-job training or a two-year degree)

Interest letters that often fit: R, C, E

Image: a truck, a train, and a plane.

## Teacher key

There is no right top three. The match sheet is scored complete, partial, or missing: complete means 12 or more clusters marked with a two-word reason and three top-three sentences that each name an interest, a strength, or a quiz letter.

Practice sort for Step 2 (the do now jobs): bus driver, 16 (Transportation, Distribution and Logistics); sandwich maker, 9 (Hospitality and Tourism); school nurse, 8 (Health Science); cashier, 14 (Marketing) or 4 (Business Management and Administration; accept either with a reason); mail carrier, 7 (Government and Public Administration); veterinarian, 1 (Agriculture, Food and Natural Resources), not 8.

The eight FACS-related clusters are 1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, and 14. The interest letters on each poster are the letters most often linked to that cluster's typical jobs; they are a guide, not a rule, and a student who marks Yes against the letters with a real reason is doing the task right.
