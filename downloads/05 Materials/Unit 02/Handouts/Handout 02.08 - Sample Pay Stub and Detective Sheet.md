## Part C: Be the detective

Show your math in the boxes.

1. Gross pay. Hours x rate = gross.

______ hours x $______ = $______ Does it match line 1? yes / no

2. Total deductions. Add lines 2, 3, 4, and 5.

$______ + $______ + $______ + $______ = $______

3. Net pay. Gross minus total deductions.

$______ minus $______ = $______ Does it match line 6? yes / no

4. Percent taken out. Total deductions divided by gross, times 100. Round to a whole number.

$______ divided by $______ = ______ x 100 = ______ percent

(Grade 6 short version: do items 1, 2, and 3 and questions 5 and 6 only.)

## Teacher key

Part A: any six, for example allowance (N), chores paid by a relative (E), babysitting (E), dog walking (E), birthday money (N), selling old games (E), tips (E), interest on savings (N), hourly job (E), salary (E). Accept "allowance" as E if the student says chores are required.

Part B: federal income tax pays for national government services (defense, highways, weather service, national parks); New York State income tax pays for state services (state roads, aid to schools, state parks); Social Security, 6.2 percent, pays retired and disabled workers and their families; Medicare, 1.45 percent, pays health coverage for people 65 and over.

Part C: 1. 24 x $17.00 = $408.00, yes. 2. $24.48 + $12.24 + $25.30 + $5.92 = $67.94. 3. $408.00 minus $67.94 = $340.06, yes. 4. $67.94 divided by $408.00 = 0.1665, about 17 percent.

5. Social Security, $25.30; it is the largest number in the deductions list. 6. 26 x $340.06 = $8,841.56. 7. Yes. Two paychecks a month is about $680.12; the phone ($35) and bus pass ($60) total $95, which leaves about $585 for everything else. 8. Larger; Social Security is a fixed percent of gross, and the friend's gross is higher (30 x $17 = $510; 6.2 percent is $31.62).

Grade 8 stretch: 9. $25.30 (25.296 rounded) and $5.92 (5.916 rounded); both match. 10. $500 divided by 0.83 = $602.41; accept $600 to $605. 11. 26 paychecks; $2,000.

Exit card: any three future sources; Social Security and Medicare; net means what you keep after deductions (accept "take-home pay").
