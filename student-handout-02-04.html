Rule: a stretch goal is not wrong. It just cannot be your only goal. Every stretch goal needs a SMART goal under it.

Part 2: Sort the six

Your teacher shows six goals. Write S for SMART, ST for stretch, or V for vague.

#GoalS, ST, or V
1I will play in the NBA.
2I will raise my math quiz average from 72 to 80 by the end of the marking period by doing the practice set every night.
3I want to be healthier.
4I will save $60 by December 1 by putting $5 a week from my allowance in the jar.
5I will own my own restaurant.
6I will try harder in school.

Part 3: Fix the vague goal

Each goal below is missing some letters. Write the missing letters, then rewrite it as a SMART goal. Use the frame if you want it: I will [what] [how much] by [date] by [how].

Fix at least three. Grade 6: two. Grade 8: all five.

#Vague goalMissing lettersMy SMART rewrite
1I want to get better at math.
2I want to be healthier.
3I want to save money.
4I want to read more.
5I want to be a better basketball player.

Partner check: trade papers. For each rewrite, circle the number and the date. If either is missing, hand it back.
