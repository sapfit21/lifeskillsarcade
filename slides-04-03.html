Slide 4

Job 1, change a light bulb

  • Switch off. Let it cool.
  • Read the label on the socket: max watts. Never go over.
  • Old bulb out counterclockwise. New bulb in clockwise until snug.
  • Pick an LED: same light, a tenth of the power

Teacher notes

Image: a lamp socket with a "60 W MAX" label visible.

Slide 5

Job 2, reset a tripped breaker

  • Open the panel door. Find the switch sitting in the middle, not on and not off.
  • Push it fully OFF. Then push it ON.
  • If it trips again right away: stop. Unplug what you just turned on. Tell an adult.
  • Dry hands. No tools in the panel. Ever.

Teacher notes

Image: a breaker panel with a row of switches, one clearly out of line with the others.

Make them say "off, then on." The middle position will not reset from the middle.

Slide 6

Job 3, unclog a drain without chemicals

  • Pull the hair out first with a plastic drain tool
  • Plunge: cover the drain, water over the cup, push and pull six times
  • Then hot water
  • Never mix chemicals. Never plunge after chemicals; they splash.

Teacher notes

Image: a bathroom sink with a plastic zip-style drain tool beside it.

Slide 7

Job 4, stop a running toilet

  • Lift the tank lid. Look, do not reach yet.
  • Is the chain caught under the flapper? Free it.
  • Is the flapper sitting flat over the hole? Reseat it.
  • Water too high? Bend the float arm down or turn the float screw.

Teacher notes

Image: the inside of a toilet tank with the flapper, chain, and float labeled.

This is the example for the six troubleshooting steps on Slide 11.

Slide 8

Jobs 5 and 6, a loose screw and hanging something

  • Screw: match the driver to the head (plus sign is Phillips, slot is flathead). Push in while turning. Stop when snug.
  • Wall: find the stud (knock, or a stud finder) or use an anchor
  • Mark with a level. Nail or screw at a slight upward angle.
  • Not where wires or pipes run: above outlets and beside faucets

Teacher notes

Image: left, two screw heads side by side; right, a level on top of a picture frame.

Slide 9

Job 7, clean a filter

  • Furnace filter: every one to three months; the arrow points toward the furnace
  • Range hood filter: monthly, in the dishwasher or hot soapy water
  • Dryer lint trap: every load. Dryer vent: yearly. Lint is a fire.
  • Switch the system off first

Teacher notes

Image: a furnace filter held up to the light, half gray with dust.

Hold up the real filter. Show the arrow.

Slide 10

Tool ID

  • Phillips screwdriver, flathead screwdriver

Teacher notes

Image: the seven tools with names under each, and under the names the Spanish words.

Pass the tray, one minute per table. Point to a tool at each table and ask one student to name it.

Slide 11

Six steps for fixing anything

  • a. What is the problem?
  • b. What should it do?
  • c. Test to find the cause
  • d. Fix it
  • e. Check that the fix worked
  • f. Stop it happening again

Teacher notes

Image: the six steps as a loop with the running toilet drawn at each step.

This is the state's troubleshooting process (CTE PSI 3. a)) in plain words. Walk the toilet through all six. Students fill Part 3.

Slide 12

Safety rules for tomorrow

  • Eyes protected at the screw station
  • No tool leaves its station
  • Nothing is plugged in
  • Dry hands at the drain station
  • One person works, the team coaches

Teacher notes

Students copy all five. These are on the clipboard tomorrow.

Slide 16

Rotation

  • Four minutes, then the reset cue: lights, countdown, hands empty
  • Initial your card only after the test
  • Safety glasses on at Station 1
  • Coach; do not grab

Teacher notes

Restate the five rules from yesterday. Start the timer.

Slide 17

The five stations

  • Station 1: drive a screw flush, then remove it
  • Station 2: seat the plunger, feel the seal
  • Station 3: measure five lines to the nearest eighth
  • Station 4: level the bracket; hang the frame straight
  • Station 5: find the tripped breaker, say the reset in order; change the bulb

Teacher notes

Image: five small photos, one per station.

Keep this up during the rotation.

Slide 18

The maintenance calendar

  • Monthly: detectors, furnace filter, range hood, lint trap every load
  • Fall: gutters, furnace check, fans reversed, hoses in
  • Winter: salt the steps, watch for ice dams
  • Spring: roof and gutters, AC test, deck check
  • Yearly: batteries, water heater drain, extinguisher gauge, ten-year detectors

Teacher notes

Students place every task on the twelve-month grid, Part 4, and justify three.

Slide 19

Argue it

  • Where do the gutters go? Why twice?
  • When do the batteries get changed?
  • Why do the hoses come in before the first freeze?

Teacher notes

Take the two placements teams disagree on most and settle them on the board.
