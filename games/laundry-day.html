Laundry Day

Seven laundry step cards appear shuffled. Tap them into order from sort to fold. Then a case card appears, a ruined garment, a set stain, or a care habit, and you tap the right answer from four.

      The ones to study:

        Worth knowing by heart:

        • The seven steps: sort, treat a stain, load, detergent, wash, dry or hang, fold or hang up
        • Heat is what ruins most garments
        • Grease needs something that breaks up oil (dish soap), a sugary drink needs an acid or plain cold water fast, ink needs alcohol
        • Daily is hang it up or hamper and check for a stain; weekly is the wash; seasonal is store it clean, never dirty, because dirt attracts insects
