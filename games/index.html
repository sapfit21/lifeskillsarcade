The Games

Short review games for the phone in your pocket or the board at the front of the room. Each one reviews one lesson. No accounts, no saving, no sound. Your score is on the screen and nowhere else.

Unit 0, Lessons 0.2 and 1.2Station RushThe sanitation routine, the handwashing steps, and the eight lab roles come as shuffled cards. Tap them into order before the clock runs out, then answer fast: whose job is it?PlayUnit 1, Lesson 1.1Hazard HuntTwelve hazards are planted in a kitchen. Then sort ten expense cards into Need or Want and get through the month in the black.PlayUnit 3, Lessons 3.12 and 3.6Green Flag, Red FlagA friend, a teammate, a cousin, a person in a group chat. Place the furniture, keep the door clear and a 3-foot path open, then name the principle behind each placement.PlayUnit 5, Lessons 5.3 and 5.4Fiber DetectiveA fiber or a clue: natural or manufactured, and which one? Teacher note

Each game names the lesson it reviews at the top of its page. They run on any phone or the classroom board, work offline once loaded, and keep nothing: no accounts, no scores saved, no cookies. To add a game, drop a fragment and a manifest entry in _tools/games and rebuild.
