Teacher note

Reviews Lesson 2.14, Lesson 2.19. Reviews Lesson 2.14 Day 1 (the Company Cards sort and the risk ladder from the board; Round 1) and Lesson 2.19 Day 1 (the reveal from kit page 5 and the rest of the story; Round 2). Do not use Round 2 before Lesson 2.19; 'what happened next' is the reveal, and the cards say nobody learns it until Day 9. Round 1 works any day of the Gilded Age Market as a warm-up before the round's trading. Round 2 is the review before the Unit 2 Test, and it also works in Unit 7 when the same instruments come back. Settings: grade 6 plays the five-card sort from the lesson (BANK, GAS, RAIL, AUTO, RADIO) in Round 1; the middle four rungs (OIL, STEEL, RAIL, ELEC) accept any order among themselves, as the Company Cards teacher key does, and the feedback names the key order.
