Color Mixer

      The ones to study:

        Worth knowing by heart:

        • Primaries: red, yellow, blue. Secondaries: orange, green, purple. Warm: red, orange, yellow. Cool: blue, green, purple.
        • Tint: a color plus white. Shade: a color plus a touch of a dark color.
        • A tint on a big wall keeps visual weight low; a shade on one wall makes a focal point.
        • The color swatches in Round 2 are drawn by the game in CSS, not as pictures, so orange and purple can show as themselves.
