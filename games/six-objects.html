Teacher note

Reviews Lesson 0.1. Reviews Lesson 0.1, the year map. Round 1 is the tray of six objects from day 1 matched to the six unit names; round 2 sorts the things students do in each unit (the six activities from Step 1 and the home skills from the do now) into the same six bins. Use it as the do now on day 2 or day 3 of the year, and again on the first day of any new unit to reset the map. the miss feedback gives the plain unit name and the NYS module name in parentheses, the same pairing as the handout key. The four home skills are sorted by what they obviously are (watch a little kid is Growing Up, Getting Along); the lesson lists them as examples without naming a unit for each, so if a student argues that fix something could be two units, they are right, which is why that one is left out.
