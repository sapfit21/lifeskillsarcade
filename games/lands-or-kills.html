Teacher note

Reviews Lesson 7.9, Lesson 7.10, Lesson 7.11. Reviews the delivery side of Lesson 7.9 (the Lands and Kills list from the teacher key, the hook rule, the four real asks a school could say yes to, and the revision rule that protects the money and the ask), plus the delivery lines on the Lesson 7.10 peer review sheet and the Lesson 7.11 judge sheet. It does not review the six-part structure, which is Pitch Perfect. Use it between Lesson 7.9 and the Lesson 7.10 Day 2 peer review round, so reviewers walk in with the criteria already in their heads; the Lands and Kills wording here is the expected list from the key, and a class that wrote a different list should play with the teacher naming the overlap. Grade 6 plays round 1 only.
