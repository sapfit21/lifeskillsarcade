      The ones to study:

        Worth knowing by heart:

        • The layering rule, three parts: (1) vary the scale, so no two patterns are the same size; (2) repeat one color through every piece; (3) let one pattern lead and keep the others quiet.
        • Texture is how a surface feels, or how it looks like it would feel. Actual texture is what your hand finds. Visual texture is what your eye is told by a flat surface.
        • A room with three colors and no texture feels flat, plain, cold, or unfinished.
