Money Math

A card shows one money problem and four answers. Tap the right one. First simple interest, the minimum payment trap, and compound growth. Then percent return and the two diversification sums.

How to play

  • I = P x r x t (interest = amount borrowed x rate as a decimal x years). Rates as decimals: 5 percent = 0.05. 15 percent = 0.15. 24 percent = 0.24.
  • Percent return = (ending value minus starting value) divided by starting value, times 100. A loss gets a minus sign. A gain gets a plus sign.
