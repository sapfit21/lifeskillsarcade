Where Clothes Go

Eight journey cards for one sweatshirt appear shuffled. Tap them into order: the three steps every garment takes first, then the five ways out. Then a move a person can make appears, and you tap its bin.

How to play

  • The sorting center comes before everything, and most donated clothing is not handed directly to a neighbor.
  • LANDFILL OR BURNED is the only true end. SHREDDED INTO FIBER and CUT INTO WIPING RAGS end the garment but not the material. SOLD ON THE FLOOR and BALED AND SOLD loop back to WORN.
  • A shirt with a cracked graphic and a hole in it reaches CUT INTO WIPING RAGS or SHREDDED INTO FIBER first, or LANDFILL if it is in bad enough shape. It will not sell on the floor.
  • No one of these fixes the system, and all four are inside a student's control this week.
