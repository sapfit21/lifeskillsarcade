      The ones to study:

        Worth knowing by heart:

        • Baking powder and baking soda both make carbon dioxide gas when they get wet and hot. The gas makes bubbles inside the batter. The bubbles get trapped and push the batter up. The heat of the oven sets the batter around the bubbles, so the bread stays risen after it cools.
        • Baking soda needs an acid, and baking powder does not, because baking powder already has its own acid mixed in with it.
        • Four bowls, one difference, no cheating.
