Teacher note

Reviews Lesson 1.5. Reviews Lesson 1.5 Day 1 (the tool ID stations and the which-tool cards) and the Tools Check. Use it the day before the Tools and Measuring Performance Check (Lesson 1.8 Day 1) or as the review tray for anyone who missed items on the Tools Check. Round 1 shows a tool picture and four names from the word bank; round 2 shows a job and four tool pictures. A miss names the tool and its job in the key's words. Grade 6 setting: round 1 uses the twelve-tool set (drop can opener, peeler, grater, food thermometer, saucepan, and the second bowl) and round 2 drops the peeler and colander jobs.

Tool ID

A tool picture appears. Tap its name from four choices. Then a job appears, and you tap the tool for the job from four tool pictures. A miss names the tool and its job.
