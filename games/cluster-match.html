Teacher note

Reviews Lesson 2.21, Lesson 2.22. Reviews Lesson 2.21 (Round 1: one job from each of the sixteen posters, matched to its cluster; the feedback line is the poster's 'what this cluster is' sentence) and Lesson 2.22 (Round 2: the six paths after high school from Part 1 of Handout 02.22 and the jobs the teacher key lists for each path). Use Round 1 as the do now of Lesson 2.22 before the research card, and both rounds as review for Unit 2 Test items 19 and 20; Round 1 also works in Unit 7 before the career plan. Settings: Round 1 draws three wrong choices from the other 15 clusters each time, so a replay reshuffles the distractors; grade 6 plays Round 1 with the eight FACS-related clusters plus four others and Round 2 with the six clue cards only.
