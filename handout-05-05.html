Fifteen rows, because there are fifteen days with needles and pins out: the eleven sewing days of Topic 5.2 (days 121 to 131) and the four project build days (days 137 to 140). Day 141 is the gallery and nothing sharp comes out, so it has no row.

Shears, every day the shears are on the table: two per station kit, six in the room. They are counted with the needles and pins and go back in the kit blades closed. If a pair is missing, the station does not leave either.

If the count does not match: nobody leaves this station. Look on the table, on the chairs, in the work, on the floor under the table, and in every pocket and cuff. If it is still missing, hand this sheet to the teacher, stand back, and the teacher sweeps with the magnet. Write what happened in the notes.
