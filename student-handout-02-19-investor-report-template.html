Name: ______________________ Date: __________ Period: ______ Investor number: ______

This is the report you turn in for the Gilded Age Market (Project 02). Fill every box. Your sources are your Investor Journal, your Paper Ledger (the price table, the holdings tables and the trade log), your decision grid, and your Return Math handout. Nothing is written from memory. Staple the journal, the ledger, the grid, and the Return Math handout behind this report when you turn it in.

You may write it by hand, type it, say it out loud while a partner writes it and then check it, or record a voice note and write the numbers here. The seven parts are the same in every form.
