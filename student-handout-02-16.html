Questions

  1. Which portfolio would you want on a bad week? Why (use a number)?
  1. Which portfolio would you want on a good week? Why (use a number)?
  1. You do not know which week is coming. That is the real situation. Which portfolio do you pick, and what are you giving up to pick it?
  1. Portfolio B never lost and never gained. What is one reason a real person might still want part of their money there?
