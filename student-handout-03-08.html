Name: ______________________________ Date: ______________ Period: ______

Directions: Page 1 is the test sheet. Write your baseline number from the do now. A baseline is your starting number, before any tool. Then test six tools. After each one, rate your stress from 1 (calm) to 5 (a lot). Write one word for how it felt. Honest numbers only. "It did nothing" is a true and useful answer. You may sit out any tool. If you do, write "sat out" and a one-word reason. Page 2 is your toolkit card. Cut it off and keep it. The test sheet is turned in. The card is yours.
