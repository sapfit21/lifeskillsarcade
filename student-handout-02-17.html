The five-step decision model

Picture cues: a question mark (name the choice), a list (list options), a scale (weigh each against what matters), a check mark (decide), a mirror (look back).

Step 1: Name the choice

The choice I am making is what to do with my ______________________ (the company or companies that rose most, or the cash I am holding).

Step 2: List the options

OptionWhat it means today
SELLSell some or all of a company that rose and hold the cash, or move it to the bank.
HOLDKeep everything I have. Do not trade. Wait.
BUYBuy more of a company with the cash I already have.
BUY ON MARGINBorrow from the broker, up to my cash, and buy more with it. Pay it back next round in cash.
