Grade 6: fill the 2-year row only. IEP and 504: any two rows.

Grade 8 stretch: What the brain is doing. In the first three years, the brain builds connections between its cells faster than at any other time. The connections that get used stay; the ones that do not get trimmed. Talking, holding, sleep, and real food are what feed that. Write two sentences: what does this mean for someone caring for a 1-year-old?
