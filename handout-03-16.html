Part 3: Readiness, at a general level

This is not about whether or when anyone should have a child. It is about what a person needs to have ready to do the eight jobs well.

What a person needsOne reason, from the clock
Age (a body and a brain that are done growing; their own school years finished)
Money (the clock has a cost)
Support (a partner, a parent, a friend, a neighbor; nobody does 24 hours alone for long)
Patience (the 2 a.m. fever and the spilled cup are the job, not the exception)
