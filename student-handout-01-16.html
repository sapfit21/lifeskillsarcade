The rule

Unit price = price divided by quantity.

Write the dollar sign on every unit price answer. If there is no dollar sign, you divided the wrong way.

The units have to match before you compare. 1 pound (lb) = 16 ounces (oz). To change pounds to ounces, multiply by 16.

Worked example: a 24 oz jar of sauce costs $2.29. $2.29 divided by 24 = $0.095 per oz.

Part C: Sales tax on non-food items

Copy the rule from the board:

In New York, most grocery food is ______ taxed. Non-food items (foil, paper plates, cups, soap) and candy, soda, and prepared hot food ______ taxed. The rate in our county is about ______ percent.

Circle the items that get taxed at the register: a bag of rice, a roll of foil, a two-liter soda, paper plates, a loaf of bread, a candy bar.

A roll of foil costs $3.49. The tax at 8.625 percent is $______. The total for the foil is $______.

Part D: The bulk trap

A 10 lb bag of potatoes costs $5.99. A 3 lb bag costs $2.99.

Unit price of the 10 lb bag: $______ per lb. Unit price of the 3 lb bag: $______ per lb. Cheaper per pound: ______.

This family eats potatoes about twice a month, about 2 pounds each time. Potatoes keep for two to three weeks in a cool, dark place, and this family keeps them in a warm kitchen cabinet.

Which bag should this family buy? ______

Why? Use the sentence starter: "The big bag is cheaper per ____________, but this family should buy the ____________ because ____________."
