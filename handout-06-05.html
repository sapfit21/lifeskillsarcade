Step 2. Criteria and constraints

Criteria (what the new design has to achieve):

  1. The food still gets protected: ____________________________________________
  2. The waste goes down: ______________________________________________________

Constraints (rules the design cannot break). These are given to you:

  1. The food has to survive shipping in a truck and being stacked three high.
  2. The food has to last at least two weeks on a store shelf.
  3. The package has to say what is inside and carry a Nutrition Facts panel.
